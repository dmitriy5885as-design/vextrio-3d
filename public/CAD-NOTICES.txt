The separately shipped files in cad/ are replaceable resources, not statically
linked into the editor. scripts/prepare-cad.mjs copies them without modification
from the pinned dependency. Full license texts are distributed beside them.
The CAD module runs in a dedicated Worker and is loaded only on CAD import.
It produces faceted geometry; VEXTRIO does not promise native CAD history.
